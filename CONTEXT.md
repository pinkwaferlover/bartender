# Dionysus: glossary

Words as the redesign uses them (grill of 22 November 2026). A glossary only: no implementation.

**The bar**: the home scene. Every screen is reached by zooming in from it, and going back zooms out to it.

**Dionysus**: the barman behind the bar. You talk to him to search, tap him for a story, and ask him for a barman's choice.

**Barman's choice**: one drink, picked at random from what you can make now. Formerly "Surprise me".

**Touchable**: anything the user can interact with. Touchable things keep their true colour; the rest of the bar is muted. Dionysus touches only touchable things, except guests.

**Guest**: another patron at the bar. Dionysus can serve and talk to guests; the user cannot touch them.

**Shelf**: the row of bottles behind Dionysus, showing your Bottles.

**Bottle**: one thing you own, named as on its label (Springbank 15).

**Style**: what a recipe calls for, from a fixed list (coastal lightly peated malt, triple sec, Campari). A bottle with no stand-in is its own Style. Swaps are between Styles.

**Family**: the broad kind of spirit (whisky, rum, gin), used to arrange the shelf and to browse by spirit.

**Barware**: glasses and tools you own (shaker tin, strainer, muddler, bar spoon).

**Feel**: what kind of drink you're in the mood for. The word "craving" is not used. (Its exact choices are still open.)
_Avoid_: craving, mood, vibe.
