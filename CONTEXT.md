# Dionysus: glossary

Words as the redesign uses them (grill of 22 November 2026). A glossary only: no implementation.

**The bar**: the home scene. Every screen is reached by zooming in from it, and going back zooms out to it.

**Dionysus**: the barman behind the bar. You talk to him to search, tap him for a story, and ask him for a barman's choice.

**Barman's choice**: one drink, picked at random from what you can make now. Formerly "Surprise me".

**Touchable**: anything the user can interact with. Touchable things keep their true colour; the rest of the bar is muted. Dionysus touches only touchable things, except patrons.

**Patron**: another customer in the bar scene. Dionysus can serve and talk to patrons; the user cannot touch them.
_Avoid_: guest (ambiguous with Visitor).

**Visitor**: a real person using someone else's bar through the app, e.g. a friend choosing a drink at your home.

**Shelf**: the row of bottles behind Dionysus, showing your Bottles.

**Bottle**: one thing you own, named as on its label (Springbank 15).

**Style**: what a recipe calls for, from a fixed list (coastal lightly peated malt, triple sec, Campari). A bottle with no stand-in is its own Style. Swaps are between Styles.

**Family**: the broad kind of spirit (whisky, rum, gin), used to arrange the shelf and to browse by spirit.

**Barware**: glasses and tools you own (shaker tin, strainer, muddler, bar spoon).

**Feel**: the taste of the drink you're in the mood for: sweet and rich, sharp and fresh, bitter, or dry and boozy. The by-feel menu and Dionysus use the same four.
_Avoid_: craving, mood, vibe.

**Moment**: when the drink is for: before dinner, with food, after dinner, or just because.

**Strength**: how much alcohol: none, low, regular or strong. "None" covers mocktails, alcohol-free beer and de-alcoholised wine.

**Look**: cosmetic settings for Dionysus: his finish (painted marble, white marble, living) and his coverage (classical, modest, cheeky). Cheeky is adult and off until switched on.
