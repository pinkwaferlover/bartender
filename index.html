For reviewers (never shown in the app)

  • Drag the drink lists sideways: they follow the finger, carry momentum and settle on a drink.
  • A–Z: run a finger or the mouse round the ring; the letters under it swell. Let go on a letter.
  • The dice: hover to jiggle, tap to roll into a random drink you can make. They also sit on drink lists and recipes.
  • Your bar: tap a bottle to mark it run out; "Load my bar" reads your own file on this device only.

What came from where

  • Colours and the DIONYSUS wordmark: Halo. Type sets A, B, C and the serif/sans mix are switchable above.
  • Home layout and feel pictures: Pour. Drink pictures: Orbit. A–Z ring: Orbit, with a new centre.
  • Recipe: Pour's layout on a strict grid; steps across the bottom third, swiping when they don't fit.
  • Prep time is a pie against the longest recipe. Shake and stir show their seconds; there is no countdown.

Held back for now

  • The motion-lab ideas (pour on reveal, rocking glass, bubbles, steam) wait for a later round.
  • The Penicillin's steps are drawn from how a working bartender makes it: muddle, jigger each pour, shake, double strain over a rock, float the Islay.
