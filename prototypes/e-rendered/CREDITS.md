# Credits

## The panther

"Prowling Leopard 3D Model Free" by iRahulRajput (Sketchfab user `rt699448`),
<https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/prowling-leopard-3d-model-free-d02a9709556348968fef810684b4e3fa>,
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Changes: scaled, turned 12° and placed on the bar counter; rendered with the bar's lights in Blender.

The model itself (17 MB glb) is not in the repository. `blender/build_bar.py` downloads it once, from
Sketchfab model uid `d02a9709556348968fef810684b4e3fa`, into `blender/_work/models/leopard-d02a9709.glb`,
and runs offline after that. If the download fails it stops and says where to put the file.
